Privacy & Data

Last updated: July 2026

This is a personal portfolio site for Zuhaib Wani. It is intentionally simple, and it collects no personal data whatsoever. This page explains exactly what that means.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no contact form, no analytics, and no tracking on this site. Nothing you do while browsing this portfolio is recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.

How to contact Zuhaib

The Contact section links directly to a mailto: link (which opens your own email client) and to LinkedIn. Any message you send that way happens entirely within your own email provider or LinkedIn's platform — it never passes through this website or any server Zuhaib controls. Their own privacy policies govern that exchange, not this one.

Cookies & analytics

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts of any kind, first-party or third-party. Nothing follows you around the web because of a visit here.

Local storage

The site remembers two preferences on your own device only — whether you've switched to light theme, and whether you've turned sound effects on — using your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device and is not accessible to Zuhaib or anyone else.

Questions
