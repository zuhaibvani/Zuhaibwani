Behind the work
About

Visuals built to survive scrutiny.

I'm from Sopore, a small town in North Kashmir — a place better known for apple orchards than design studios. I taught myself the craft far from any creative hub, turned curiosity into a profession, and spent seven years growing from a freelance designer into a senior creative trusted by global teams. The distance never mattered; the work did.

I specialize in technical visualization for infrastructure and engineering at global scale — translating complex systems, technical drawings, and planning proposals into visuals that hold up to engineers, architects, and funders, not just look good in a pitch deck.

That precision was built at a global engineering consultancy, delivering visualization for programmes including NEOM THE LINE, Heathrow Terminal 5, and HS2 — work where the visuals had to be technically accurate enough to support real funding and planning decisions. I've since carried that rigor into production-grade Unreal Engine 5 experiences and AI-assisted pipelines at an immersive-tech team.

I'm open to remote, hybrid and on-site roles — I've worked across all three and adapt easily. I lead process and outcomes without needing a title to do it, and hold one standard everywhere: if it doesn't survive scrutiny, it doesn't ship.

Good to know

Where is Zuhaib based, and what working styles has he handled?

He's based in Delhi NCR, India, and is open to remote, hybrid or on-site work. He started out in Sopore, North Kashmir, and has since worked on-site and hybrid across Delhi NCR and Hyderabad, and remotely with international clients — so he's comfortable across very different setups, teams and time zones.

Can you take a project from concept all the way to delivery?

Yes — that's the core of how I work. Strategy, art direction, design, 3D, motion and final production are all handled by one person, so the original idea survives intact from brief to finished asset.

What disciplines and tools do you actually cover?

Brand identity and visual systems, motion graphics and video, photoreal 3D visualization, and real-time interactive work in Unreal Engine 5 — using the full Adobe suite (Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, After Effects, Premiere Pro), Blender, Figma, Substance Painter and AI-assisted workflows.

Have you worked with international teams and clients?

Yes. I've delivered visualization for global infrastructure programmes including NEOM THE LINE, Heathrow Terminal 5, and HS2 at a global engineering consultancy, and produced real-time and 3D work for an immersive-tech team — across a range of industries, cultures and working styles.

How do you use AI in your process?

As an accelerator, not a replacement. Midjourney, Adobe Firefly and Google AI Studio for image generation; Runway, Gemini, Kling and Grok for video (Grok also for quick research); Claude, ChatGPT and Kimi as AI assistants for automation; and MCP-based workflows in Blender and Unreal Engine for scripting and Blueprint logic. All of it speeds up ideation and iteration — typically cutting concept-to-delivery time by around a third — while the final craft stays fully hand-controlled. I've also built basic Python fundamentals, enough to read, follow and lightly adapt the scripts behind these automation workflows rather than treating them as a black box. Also completed the Generative AI Mastermind course (Outskill) to stay current on the fast-moving side of this space.

What certifications have you completed?

Learn OpenUSD: Working With Prims and Default Schemas (NVIDIA), Learn OpenUSD: Learning About Stages, Prims and Attributes (NVIDIA), Generative AI Mastermind (Outskill), Unreal Engine 5 – Lighting, Fog & Post Processing, Blender & After Effects VFX Masterclass, Diploma in Graphic Designing, Leading Hybrid and Remote Teams.
